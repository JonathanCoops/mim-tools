Money in Motion Free Tools

Simple tools to help you get your money organised, one step at a time. They work on your phone, and your numbers never leave it.

  • About 2 minutes

    Which Baby Step am I on?

    A few quick questions about where your money is right now. Find out which step you're on and what to do this week.

    Take the quiz
  • About 5 minutes

    Debt Snowball Planner

    List your debts, smallest balance first, and see the month you'll be debt-free. Then download a one-page plan for the fridge.

    Plan my snowball
  • About 15 minutes

    Saving for Mission Builder

    Heading on a mission trip, gap year or church team trip? Count the real cost, see what you can save, and get a page to share with supporters.

    Build my plan
  • About 10 minutes

    Christmas Without Debt

    Plan Christmas around your paydays and pay for it in cash. Plus a kind message to suggest a cheaper Christmas to the family.

    Plan my Christmas
